# PÚRO — Prompt de logo (para ChatGPT / generador de imágenes)

> Marca: **PÚRO Vitality** · Paleta: azul acero #10243B + cobre #C2703D + hueso #F2EFE9.
> El acento de la "Ú" en cobre es el elemento de marca distintivo.

```
Design a premium, modern logo for a men's vitality supplement brand called "PÚRO".

STYLE: clean flat vector logo, minimalist, masculine, premium men's-health / pharmaceutical feel. Centered on a plain dark charcoal-navy background. Not a photo, not a 3D render — a crisp graphic logo.

COMPOSITION (stacked logo):
1) Top: an emblem badge — a steel-blue hexagon (like a bolt head / nut), with a thin copper inner outline, containing a bold uppercase letter "P" in bone-white, and a small copper spark/accent mark on its top-right.
2) Below: the wordmark "PURO" in a bold, heavy, uppercase geometric sans-serif (like Anton / Archivo Black), in bone-white, with wide letter-spacing. IMPORTANT: place a COPPER acute accent mark ( ´ ) over the letter "U", so it reads "PÚRO" — the accent is the signature brand detail, styled as a small copper spark.
3) A thin copper divider line with a small copper diamond in the center.
4) Under it the word "VITALITY" in small, wide letter-spaced copper letters.

COLORS (strict palette):
- Midnight steel blue #10243B (primary) and #1C3A5E
- Copper / burnt orange #C2703D (accent + the Ú accent)
- Bone white #F2EFE9 (text)

Vector, sharp, perfectly legible text, high contrast, professional brand identity, logo presentation. Square 1:1.
```

**Tips:** lo más difícil es el acento sobre la Ú y que escriba "PURO" bien. Si sale mal, regeneralo o pedí "keep the text exactly PURO with a copper accent over the U". Para variantes: agregá "give me 3 variations".
