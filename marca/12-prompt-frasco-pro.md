# PÚRO — Prompt frasco PRO (estilo MenLab, más natural) para ChatGPT

> Mismo nivel que la referencia MenLab (ámbar + etiqueta oscura + caja + fondo claro) pero con onda más natural (cobre + verde botánico sutil) y premium. 4:5 o 1:1.
> ⚠️ El sello "Aprobado por ANMAT" solo se puede mostrar con el RNPA ya aprobado. Si todavía no lo tenés, sacá esa línea del prompt.

```
Professional e-commerce hero photo of a premium men's supplement, MenLab-style, clean and high-end. Vertical 4:5.

LAYOUT: an amber glass supplement bottle standing in front, with its matching premium dark cardboard box standing slightly behind to the left. Soft natural shadow, clean composition.

BOTTLE: premium AMBER GLASS bottle (warm honey-amber, you can sense the capsules inside) with a matte black screw cap.

LABEL: matte deep navy-blue (#10243B) wrap-around label, premium soft-touch, with crisp legible typography and a thin copper (#C2703D) hairline frame. A very subtle embossed botanical leaf line-art in the background of the label (natural feel). Layout top to bottom:
- "PÚRO" in bold italic uppercase sans-serif, bone-white, with the acute accent over the U in copper
- descriptor in lighter type: "Vitalidad Masculina Natural"
- three small bullet lines in bone-white:
   • Formulado para hombres
   • Con Maca, Ashwagandha y Zinc
   • Energía y vitalidad natural
- bottom row: "Suplemento Dietario"   and large "60 Cápsulas"

BADGE (optional, only if already ANMAT-registered): a small circular dark seal in the top-right corner reading "APROBADO POR ANMAT".

BACKGROUND: clean seamless near-white / very light warm grey, bright and soft, smooth subtle gradient, lots of negative space. No clutter.

LIGHTING & STYLE: soft diffused daylight studio lighting, gentle warm rim-light on the amber glass, natural premium pharmacy / men's-health editorial aesthetic, trustworthy and clean, photorealistic, ultra-sharp legible label text, 4K.
```

## ✅ VERSIÓN CORREGIDA v2 (solo frasco, ámbar de verdad, acento en otro color)

```
Professional studio product photo of ONE single men's supplement bottle. ONLY the bottle — NO box, NO second product, NO props. Centered, vertical 4:5.

BOTTLE GLASS: must be real AMBER GLASS — warm honey / caramel brown, translucent, you can see the dark capsules through the glass. NOT blue, NOT navy glass. Matte black screw cap.

LABEL: a matte deep navy-blue (#10243B) wrap-around label on the amber bottle (the navy label contrasts against the warm amber glass). Thin copper hairline frame. Crisp, sharp, legible typography:
- BRAND: "PÚRO" big, bold italic uppercase sans-serif. The four letters P-U-R-O are BONE WHITE. ONLY the acute accent mark over the U is COPPER / burnt-orange (#C2703D) — it is the single accent color, the letters stay white.
- descriptor under it, copper, small: "VITALIDAD MASCULINA NATURAL"
- thin copper divider line
- three white bullet points (what it does):
   • Más energía y vitalidad
   • Apoya el metabolismo y la firmeza
   • Formulado para hombres
- ingredient line in small white text: "Maca · Ashwagandha · Zinc · Magnesio · Vitamina D"
- bottom row: "Suplemento Dietario"  +  big "60 Cápsulas"

COLOR BALANCE: three tones working together — warm amber glass + cool navy label + white text with a single copper accent. Premium and rich, NOT flat or two-tone.

BACKGROUND: clean seamless soft white / very light warm grey, bright, smooth subtle gradient, soft natural shadow under the bottle, lots of negative space, no clutter.

LIGHTING: soft diffused studio daylight, warm golden rim-light glowing through the amber glass, premium natural men's-health editorial look, photorealistic, ultra-sharp legible label text, 4K.
```

> Si igual te lo hace azul: agregá al inicio **"AMBER glass bottle, brown pharmacy glass like a beer/medicine bottle"** y al final **"the glass is amber brown, never blue"**.

## Variantes rápidas
- **Aún más natural:** cambiá la etiqueta a *"matte deep forest-green (#1C3B2E) label with copper accents and kraft-paper texture"*.
- **Para mobile/hero:** repetí en **1:1** o **4:5**, frasco centrado y un poco de aire arriba.
- **Tip de texto:** si la IA escribe mal los bullets, generá primero con solo "PÚRO / Vitalidad Masculina / 60 Cápsulas" y sumá los bullets en el diseño final.
