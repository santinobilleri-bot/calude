# NUVA — Prompt UGC (foto con beneficios, estilo referencia truehome)

UGC selfie de una mujer real estilo Caro sosteniendo la bolsa Nuva, con texto de
beneficio al costado. Apto ANMAT (energía, ansiedad, bienestar — sin adelgazar).

## Prompt para ChatGPT
```
Create a realistic UGC-style selfie photo (authentic, slightly casual, like a real customer photo taken on a phone) of a natural-looking 37-year-old Argentine woman, warm friendly smile, relatable and real (not a model), light makeup, hair in a casual ponytail, wearing a simple top, in her cozy home with a softly blurred background (a plant, warm light).

She is holding a soft blush-pink "nuva" stand-up pouch (doypack) up next to her face, facing the camera, so the front of the pouch is clearly visible. The pouch shows: the lowercase "nuva" logo in raspberry pink with a tiny berry-leaf accent, the headline "ENERGÍA DESDE ADENTRO", small benefit icons, a red-berry illustration, and a "SABOR FRUTOS ROJOS" band. Sugar-free, vegan red-berry gummies.

On the LEFT side of the image, add clean bold text overlay in soft white/dark, three short lines:
"más energía,
menos ansiedad,
desde adentro."

Warm, natural, feminine, real, aspirational-but-relatable. Soft natural light, photorealistic, vertical format, 4K. Make sure the brand text reads exactly "nuva".
```

## Variaciones del texto overlay
- "más energía, menos ansiedad, desde adentro."
- "tu envión de cada día"
- "sin dietas. sin azúcar. sin culpa."
- "energía y calma en una gomita."
- "para mujeres como vos."

## Tips
- Formato vertical 9:16 (reels/stories).
- Un poco casual = más credible (UGC).
- Generar 3-4 con distintas mujeres/edades (35, 40, 45) para rotar.
